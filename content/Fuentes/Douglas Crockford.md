**Douglas Crockford** is an American [computer programmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_programmer "Computer programmer") who is involved in the development of the [JavaScript](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JavaScript "JavaScript") language. He specified the data format [JSON](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JSON "JSON") (JavaScript Object Notation), and has developed various JavaScript related tools such as the static code analyzer [JSLint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JSLint "JSLint") and minifier [JSMin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JSMin "JSMin").[^1] He wrote the book *JavaScript: The Good Parts*, published in 2008, followed by *How JavaScript Works* in 2018. He was a senior JavaScript architect at [PayPal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PayPal "PayPal") until 2019, and is also a writer and speaker on JavaScript, JSON, and related [web technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_technologies "Web technologies").
